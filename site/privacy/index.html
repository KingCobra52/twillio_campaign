Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Siddarth Thota Personal Alerts handles information connected to its SMS reminder program and to this website.

Effective date: September 14, 2026 · Last updated: September 14, 2026

Who this policy covers

Siddarth Thota Personal Alerts is operated by Siddarth Thota, an individual in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, United States. The program sends personal reminder text messages to a single recipient, who is the operator. This policy covers that SMS program and this website.

This website publishes disclosures only. It has no signup form, no login, no comment feature, and no backend service. Nothing on this site collects information from visitors. Details of the messaging program itself are on the SMS Program Disclosure page.

Information collected

Information related to the SMS program

  • Mobile phone number. The single mobile number enrolled in the program, which belongs to the operator.
  • Consent record. A record that the enrolled number opted in, including when opt-in was recorded and the wording of the disclosure that was shown at the time.
  • Message content and metadata. The text of reminder messages sent, the date and time each message was sent, and delivery information returned by the messaging provider, such as delivered, queued, or failed status and any error code.
  • Inbound replies. Replies sent to the program's number, including keyword replies such as STOP, START, and HELP.
  • Opt-out state. A record of whether the enrolled number is currently subscribed or has opted out, and when that changed.

Information related to this website

  • None collected by the operator. This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics, embeds no trackers, loads no third-party fonts or scripts, and stores nothing in the browser.
  • Standard hosting logs. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may record standard technical request information such as IP addresses and user-agent strings for security and abuse prevention. That information is collected and controlled by GitHub under its own privacy practices, and the operator does not receive, request, or have access to it.
  • Email you send. If you email the published support address, the operator receives the message, your email address, and whatever you choose to include in it.

How information is used

Information is used only for operating the reminder program and for meeting legal and carrier obligations. Specifically:

  • To send the reminder messages the recipient has asked to receive.
  • To honor STOP, START, and HELP replies, and to keep the opt-out state accurate so messages are not sent to a number that has opted out.
  • To troubleshoot delivery problems, such as investigating a message that failed or arrived late.
  • To keep records of consent and opt-out that the messaging provider and mobile carriers may require, and that applicable law may require.
  • To respond to support email sent to the published support address.

Information is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling, scoring, automated decision-making, or resale. The program does not build audience segments and does not enrich its data with information bought from other sources.

Sharing and service providers

The program relies on two providers. Each receives only what it needs to do its job, and each acts as a service provider to the operator, not as an independent recipient free to use the data for its own purposes.

Service providers and what they process
Provider Role What it processes
Twilio Messaging provider that transmits SMS to and from mobile carriers. The enrolled mobile number, outbound message content, inbound replies, delivery status, and related timestamps.
GitHub (GitHub Pages) Static hosting for this website. Standard web request information for visitors to this site, under GitHub's own control. No program data is stored on this site.

Mobile carriers also necessarily handle message content and the recipient's number in order to deliver a text message to a handset. That is inherent to how SMS works and is outside the operator's control.

Beyond those providers, information is disclosed only where the operator reasonably believes disclosure is required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or is necessary to investigate or address suspected fraud, abuse, or a security incident.

No sale or marketing sharing of mobile data

Mobile numbers and SMS consent data are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing, advertising, promotional, or lead-generation purposes. The only sharing that occurs is with the service providers listed above, strictly for the purpose of delivering the messages the recipient asked for. The program has no affiliates and no advertising partners.

How long information is kept

Information is kept only as long as there is an operational, legal, or compliance reason to keep it, and it is deleted when that reason ends. In practice:

  • Opt-out records are kept indefinitely. A record that a number opted out has to be kept so the program can continue to honor that opt-out. Deleting it would risk messaging a number that asked not to be messaged.
  • Consent records are kept for as long as the number is enrolled, and for a reasonable period afterward so the operator can demonstrate that consent existed if a carrier, Twilio, or a regulator asks.
  • Message content and delivery logs are kept only as long as needed for troubleshooting and compliance, and are then deleted. Copies also sit in Twilio's systems and are retained according to Twilio's own retention practices and account settings, which the operator configures but does not fully control.
  • Support email is kept as long as needed to resolve the matter it concerns, and then deleted in the ordinary course.

These periods are stated conservatively on purpose. Where a specific retention period is not fixed above, the operator keeps the information for no longer than is reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected for.

Access and deletion requests

Anyone whose information is in the program may ask what is held about them, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Send the request to hellotheking52@gmail.com, and the operator will respond within a reasonable period.

Two limits apply, and they apply to every request:

  • Opt-out records are retained even after a deletion request, because they are what stops the program from messaging a number again. This is the narrowest exception the operator can apply while still honoring an opt-out.
  • Records the operator is required to keep by law, or that Twilio or a mobile carrier requires be kept for compliance, are retained for as long as that requirement lasts.

Data held by Twilio may need a separate request to Twilio, and data in GitHub's hosting logs is subject to GitHub's own processes. The operator will say clearly which case applies when responding.

Security and its limits

The operator takes reasonable precautions: access to the messaging account is protected by a strong, unique password and multi-factor authentication, provider credentials are never committed to this website's source repository, and the number of people with access is limited to the operator alone.

Those precautions have real limits, and it is better to state them plainly than to imply a guarantee:

  • SMS is not a secure channel. Text messages are not end-to-end encrypted. They pass through carrier networks and are stored on the receiving handset. Anyone with access to the handset can read them. Sensitive information should not be sent by SMS.
  • No system is perfectly secure. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely safe, and the operator cannot guarantee absolute security of any information.
  • Provider security is outside the operator's control. Information held by Twilio, GitHub, and mobile carriers is protected by those companies' own security practices.

Children

This program is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from them. The program's single recipient is an adult. If the operator learns that information from a child under 13 has been collected, it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the program changes. When it is, the revised policy is published at this same address and the "Last updated" date at the top of the page is changed. Material changes to how information is collected, used, or shared will be described in the policy itself rather than made silently. Continuing to receive messages after a change takes effect means accepting the updated policy; a recipient who does not accept it can reply STOP at any time.

Prior versions of this policy are recorded in the public version history of the repository that publishes this website.
