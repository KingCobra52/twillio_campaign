6. Opting out and resubscribing

Reply STOP to any message to stop all messages from the program. Related keywords including END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, and QUIT are treated the same way. The program sends one confirmation message acknowledging the opt-out, and then sends nothing further.

To resume messages after opting out, reply START. Related keywords including UNSTOP and YES are treated the same way. Resubscribing is a new opt-in and is confirmed with a single message.

Opting out can also be requested by email at hellotheking52@gmail.com. Replying STOP is faster and takes effect immediately. Keywords are not case sensitive.
